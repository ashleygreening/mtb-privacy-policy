1. Terms of Service

1.1 Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account or using Meet The Band, you agree to this User Agreement. If you do not agree, you may not use Meet The Band's social or matching features.

1.2 Eligibility

  • Meet The Band is not intended for users under 14.
  • Users aged 14–17 may only be matched with other users in the same age group.
  • Users aged 18 and over may only be matched with other adults.
  • Age verification uses Apple's Declared Age Range API to determine whether your age is Assured by Apple or Self-declared.

1.3 Account Registration

To use social features, you must create an account with:

  • Username
  • Password (stored securely using a one-way hashing algorithm)
  • Name
  • Date of birth
  • Preferred contact method

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials.

1.4 App Features

Daily Matches

You receive one match per day. Matches are based on age group and profile information.

Chat Consent

Chat only opens when both users explicitly consent to chat.

Contact Sharing Consent

Preferred contact information is shared only when both users agree after sending at least one message.

Chat Limits

Each user may have up to five active chats at a time.

Blocking

Blocking a user prevents future matches.

Reporting

Reports are sent to us for review and stored securely. Reporting also blocks the reported user for the reporter.

1.5 User Conduct

You agree not to:

  • Harass, threaten, or harm other users
  • Impersonate others
  • Upload harmful, malicious, or deceptive content
  • Attempt to bypass age restrictions
  • Use unsupported or unsafe external links
  • Engage in spam or solicitation

Violations may result in suspension or removal.

1.6 Supported External Links

To protect users from malicious content, Meet The Band only supports links from: Amazon, Audiomack, TikTok, Instagram, Bandcamp, Apple Music, YouTube, Spotify, and SoundCloud.

1.7 User Content, Ownership & Authenticity

You retain any ownership rights you have in the content you create and share on Meet The Band. Uploading a profile photo, adding a biography, or linking to music or other content does not transfer ownership of that content to Meet The Band.

By submitting content or adding external links to your profile, you confirm that the information is accurate, that you have the rights or permission necessary to share it, and that your use of the content does not violate another person's rights.

You must not impersonate another person, artist, band, or organization, or falsely claim an identity, affiliation, or ownership of music or other content that is not yours.

By submitting content to Meet The Band, you grant Meet The Band a non-exclusive, royalty-free permission to host, display, and make that content available as necessary to operate the app and provide its features. This permission is limited to those purposes and does not transfer ownership of your content to Meet The Band.

External links remain subject to the terms and policies of the relevant third-party services. Meet The Band does not claim ownership of content hosted by those services.

1.8 Suspension & Termination

Violations of this User Agreement, including the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Community Guidelines, may result in enforcement action.

Depending on the nature and seriousness of the violation, we may issue a warning, restrict access to features, suspend an account, or permanently terminate an account by deleting it from Meet The Band.

2. Privacy Policy

2.1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information. By using Meet The Band, you consent to these practices.

2.2 Information We Collect

Account Information (Required)

  • Username
  • Password (hashed; we cannot view it)
  • Name
  • Date of birth
  • Preferred contact method

Profile Information (Optional)

  • Bio
  • Goals
  • Favorite genres
  • Instruments played
  • Music production/composition tools
  • Profile photo
  • General location (country and state if applicable
  • Supported external links

Age Verification

We use Apple's Declared Age Range API to determine whether your age is:

  • Assured by Apple, or
  • Self-declared

Social Interaction Data

  • Daily match assignments
  • Chat consent status
  • Contact-sharing consent
  • Chat messages
  • Chat limits and activity

Safety & Moderation Data

  • Reports (reason and description)
  • Blocks

Technical Data

  • Device type
  • App version
  • Diagnostic logs

We do not collect advertising identifiers or tracking data.

2.3 How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To generate daily matches
  • To enable chat when both users consent
  • To share preferred contact information only when both users agree
  • To enforce age restrictions
  • To maintain safety and review reports
  • To improve app functionality

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

2.4 When We Share Information

With Other Users

  • Profile information
  • Chat messages
  • Preferred contact information (only with mutual consent)

With Service Providers

For secure hosting, database storage, and error logging.

For Legal Reasons

If required to comply with law or protect user safety.

2.5 Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL database.
  • Passwords are hashed; we cannot view them.
  • Access is restricted to authorized personnel.
  • Industry-standard security measures protect your data.

2.6 Your Controls

  • Edit or remove optional profile fields
  • Block users
  • Report users
  • Request account deletion

2.7 Children & Age Restrictions

  • Users under 14 cannot create profiles or use social features.
  • Users aged 14–17 are matched only with others in the same age group.
  • Users aged 18 and over are matched only with adults.

We do not knowingly collect data from users under 14.

2.8 Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for:

  • Providing the service
  • Safety and moderation
  • Legal compliance

We may update this Privacy Policy. Significant changes will be communicated in the app.

3. Community Guidelines

These guidelines help keep Meet The Band safe, respectful, and enjoyable.

3.1 Be Honest

  • Provide accurate age information
  • Do not impersonate others
  • Do not misrepresent your identity or intentions

3.2 Be Respectful

  • No harassment, bullying, or hate speech
  • No sexual content involving minors
  • No threats or intimidation

3.3 Be Safe

  • Do not share unsafe or unsupported links
  • Do not attempt to bypass chat or contact-sharing consent
  • Report harmful behavior immediately

3.4 Be Appropriate

  • Keep conversations respectful
  • Do not spam or solicit
  • Do not use Meet The Band for commercial promotion unless explicitly allowed

3.5 Protect Yourself

  • Share your preferred contact only when you feel comfortable
  • Use blocking if someone makes you uncomfortable
  • Report violations so we can take action

3.6 Violations & Enforcement

Violations of these Community Guidelines or any other part of this User Agreement may result in enforcement action.

Depending on the circumstances, consequences may include:

  • A warning
  • Restrictions on access to app features
  • Temporary account suspension
  • Permanent account termination and deletion

We may take immediate action for serious violations. We reserve the right to remove accounts that violate this Agreement or pose a risk to the safety of other users.
